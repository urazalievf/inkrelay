## r/eink, r/Onyx_Boox, r/Supernote, r/RemarkableTablet, Viwoods community

**Title:** How do you get handwritten notes from your e-ink tablet into Apple Notes, OneNote or Notion?

I write everything on an e-ink tablet, but my notes live in Obsidian and Apple Notes, and the tablet only syncs to its own cloud or a Drive folder. I'm building a small Mac app that watches that folder (or reads the tablet's cloud directly), reads the handwriting on the Mac, and files each notebook into the notes app you already use, with the page images next to the text. Tasks you write become reminders, and planner pages go to your calendar.

Two questions, if you have a minute:

1. Which tablet do you use, and where do you want the notes to end up? (Apple Notes, Obsidian, OneNote, Notion, Google Docs, something else)
2. How do you move them there today?

## r/ObsidianMD

**Title:** Handwritten notes from any e-ink tablet into Obsidian, with your edits kept

Most tablet → Obsidian plugins cover one brand and overwrite the note on every sync. I'm working on a Mac app that takes notes from any tablet (Viwoods, reMarkable, Supernote, Boox, Kindle Scribe), reads the handwriting on-device, and writes Markdown plus the original PDF into your vault. It only rewrites its own block between two markers, so whatever you add above or below stays. Written tasks become Apple Reminders, and there's a read-later review stack of new pages.

What would you want from it that the current plugins don't do? Waitlist: <LINK>
