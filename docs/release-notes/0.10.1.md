The first public download of InkRelay for Mac.

- InkRelay checks this page once a day and tells you when a newer version is out. Nothing about your Mac is sent.
- Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Universal for Apple silicon and Intel; macOS 14 or later.
- New command: inkrelay measure scores how well your handwriting is read, on pages you pick and correct once, so you can tell whether a change helps.

InkRelay files the handwriting from your e-ink tablet (Viwoods, Supernote, reMarkable, Boox, Kindle Scribe) into Obsidian, Apple Notes and Calendar, Google, Microsoft and Notion. Free for folder exports, Obsidian and the Apple apps; Pro is $29 once, with a 14-day trial.
